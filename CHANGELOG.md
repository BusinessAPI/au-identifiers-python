# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-24

First release.

- ABN, ACN and ARBN: validate, normalise and format
- TFN: validate and normalise, including 8-digit organisation TFNs and the individual (9-digit) option
- Director ID: format check (15 digits starting with 036)
- VIN: format check (17 characters, no I, O or Q)
